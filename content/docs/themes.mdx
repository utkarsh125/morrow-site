Morrow includes over 65 curated color themes from the official Kitty terminal ecosystem.

Press `Ctrl-T` in Morrow to open the interactive theme picker with live preview, or run `/theme <name>`. Your selection automatically persists in `~/.config/morrow/config.toml`.
